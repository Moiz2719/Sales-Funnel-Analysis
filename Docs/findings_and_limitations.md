# Findings

This project includes queries for sales-funnel counts, stage conversion rates, traffic-source comparisons, elapsed journey time, and purchase revenue measures.

## Sales funnel

- Unique users at each of the five funnel stages.
- Stage-to-stage and overall conversion percentages.

## Acquisition channels

- Counts and conversion percentages grouped by traffic source.

## Conversion timing

- Average elapsed minutes between observed page-view, add-to-cart, and purchase events.

## Revenue

- Purchase-event count, distinct purchasers, total purchase amount, and average amount per purchase event.
